Projector connection

Uses the projector's network port (PJLink). The Pi is 192.168.50.1 on that cable.

Campaign slides: the "paid for by" line

Texas law says political advertising must say it is political advertising and who paid for it. This strip is burned into everything in the campaign playlist and can't be switched off per file. Check the exact wording with the campaign's lawyer.
