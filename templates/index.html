Seasons

Date ranges that pick the show. Dates like 10-01 repeat every year; 2026-11-03 is one year only. The first match wins.

Manage videos

To make a show or convert a video, open the studio on a laptop: https://balcony.local:8443/studio/ (accept the certificate warning once). You can also drop ready files straight into the media folder on the Pi:

Hologram mode: invert turns dark-on-white artwork into glowing text on black. Knockout makes white areas black and keeps other colours. Black disappears on the mesh, so figures look like they float.

Playlists

A playlist is just a folder of videos. "Loops" playlists play round and round; "plays once" playlists play one video and then go dark.

Settings

Projector connection

Uses the projector's network port (PJLink). The Pi is 192.168.50.1 on that cable.

Sound and pictures

Quiet hours

Dim the picture late in the evening so it bothers neighbours and drivers less.

Where the balcony is

For sunset times. Dallas is about 32.78, -96.80.

Alerts on your phone

Install the free ntfy app, pick a topic name, and put https://ntfy.sh/your-topic here. You'll get a message if the show stops, the projector faults, or the Pi runs hot.

PIN

Anyone on your Wi-Fi can open this page. A PIN stops them changing things.
